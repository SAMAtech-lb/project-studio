How do I switch my cat to fresh food?

Mix it in slowly over 7 days. Your first box includes a transition guide with exact amounts for each day.

How long does the food keep?

Up to 4 days in the fridge once opened and 3 months in the freezer. Each portion is dated.

Can I skip or cancel?

Yes. Skip, pause or cancel online any time before your next box ships. No fees.

Is it suitable for kittens?

All recipes are complete for growth, so they're suitable from 8 weeks old.

Where do you deliver?

Across the mainland US in 1–3 days. Boxes stay cold for 48 hours.
