How a project works

  1. Consultation at your homeWe visit, measure, talk through how you live and agree a budget. You receive a written proposal within a week.
  2. Concept & layoutMood boards, material samples and furniture layouts, presented in our studio in Alserkal Avenue.
  3. Detailed design3D renders, joinery drawings, lighting plan and a full schedule of furniture and finishes with prices.
  4. Build, buy & installWe order everything, manage contractors and style the finished home, usually while you're away for a few days.

Questions we're often asked

What budget do I need for a full home?

As a guide, furnishing a 3-bedroom apartment (without construction) usually costs AED 250,000–450,000 plus the design fee. We'll give you a realistic budget after the consultation.

Do you work outside Dubai?

Yes, across the UAE and for some clients in Oman and Saudi Arabia. Travel is charged at cost.

Can we keep some of our furniture?

Of course. We often design around pieces our clients love and reupholster or refinish them where needed.

How long does a project take?

A single room takes 4–6 weeks to design plus delivery times. A full apartment typically takes 4–7 months including furniture lead times.

Do you handle landlord and building approvals?

Yes. For fit-outs we prepare the drawings and manage NOCs with your building management and the relevant authority.
