FAQ

Questions operations managers ask us first.

Still unsure? Our engineers will answer on the demo call — or visit a live Helio site near you.

Visit a live site
Do we need to change our floor or racking?

No. Helio robots use lidar and cameras to navigate by your existing racking and walls. We need a reasonably flat concrete floor and Wi-Fi in the zone — that's it.

Is it safe for people to work next to the robots?

Yes. Every robot is certified to ISO 3691-4, slows when people are within 2 metres and stops within 30 cm. Pickers don't need special training beyond our 90-minute session.

What happens if a robot breaks down?

The fleet re-routes its missions to other robots in seconds. On the Scale plan a technician is on site within 4 hours, and we keep spare robots at every regional hub.

How do the robots charge?

They dock themselves at chargers during quiet moments. A 12-minute top-up gives about 3 hours of work, so robots run all shifts without anyone plugging them in.

Can we return robots after peak season?

Yes. On the Scale plan you can add robots month-to-month for peak and send them back after, on top of your base fleet.
