FAQ

Common questions

Do we have to replace our current help desk?

No. You can add the Converso AI agent to Zendesk, Intercom or Gorgias and keep your current workflows. Many teams move fully to Converso later to save on seat costs.

What happens when the AI doesn't know the answer?

It tells the customer it's passing them to the team, then assigns the conversation with a summary and the steps it already took. You're not charged for hand-offs.

How long does setup take?

Connecting your help centre and switching on the agent for one channel takes about an hour. Most teams test on 10–20% of traffic for a week before rolling out fully.

Which languages are supported?

The agent replies in 45 languages, including Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Japanese and Portuguese, even if your help centre is only in English.
