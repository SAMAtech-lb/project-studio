FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a lawyer, or can I apply myself?

Many people apply successfully on their own. A lawyer is worth it when your history is complicated — a previous refusal, a gap in status, a criminal record, or a relationship IRCC might question — or when you simply can't afford a mistake.

Can you guarantee my application will be approved?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Decisions are made by IRCC officers. What I can promise is an honest assessment before you pay, and a complete, well-argued file.

I'm outside Canada. Can you still help?

Yes — about half of my clients are abroad. Consultations are by video and everything else is handled through the secure client portal.

What's the difference between a lawyer and a consultant?

Both can represent you before IRCC. Only a lawyer can represent you in Federal Court if an application is refused and you need a judicial review.

How quickly can we start?

Consultations are usually available within the week. Once you sign the retainer, you receive your document checklist the same day.
