Halden is an architecture and interior studio designing calm, enduring homes and hospitality spaces — where every material earns its place and nothing shouts.

We work across residential, hospitality and cultural projects, taking each from first sketch to the last door handle. Our buildings respond to their landscape first and to trends never.

Natural stone, oak, lime plaster and linen. A limited palette, used carefully, ages beautifully — and that's the only kind of luxury we're interested in.

How we work, from first walk to handover

Every project follows the same four stages. Clear fees, a single point of contact and no surprises on site.

I.

Listen

Site visits, briefing sessions and a long walk around the land at different times of day.

II.

Concept

Sketches, physical models and a material board — three directions narrowed down to one.

III.

Develop

Technical drawings, permits, engineering and detailed interior specification.

IV.

Build

Weekly site supervision with trusted local craftspeople until the keys are handed over.

The studio

A team of fourteen architects, designers & makers

Founded by Ingrid Halden in Oslo in 2009, the studio opened a second office in Lisbon in 2018. We take on no more than eight projects a year so each one gets our full attention.
