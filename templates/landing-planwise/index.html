FAQ

Questions from engineering leads

How does the Jira import work?

Connect your Jira Cloud site, choose the projects and Planwise imports issues, comments, attachments, labels and assignees. Most teams finish in under an hour; we keep a link back to the original Jira issue.

Can we keep using GitHub Issues for open-source work?

Yes. Two-way sync keeps public GitHub Issues and your internal Planwise team in step, including comments and status.

What counts as a user?

Anyone who is a member of your workspace. Guests who only view or comment on shared projects are free on paid plans.

Where is our data stored?

By default in the US (AWS us-east-1). Enterprise workspaces can choose EU hosting in Frankfurt (eu-central-1).

Do you offer discounts for startups or non-profits?

Startups that raised less than $5M get 50% off Standard for the first year, and registered non-profits get 50% off any paid plan.
