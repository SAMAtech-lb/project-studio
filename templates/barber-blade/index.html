Our story

Old-school craft, new-school style

Blade & Co. started with two chairs and a record player. Fourteen years later we've got six chairs, the same record player and a crew of barbers who treat every cut like their reputation depends on it — because it does.

No rushing, no upselling. Just a proper cut, a good conversation and a hot towel to finish.

Vincent MoreauFounder & Master Barber
