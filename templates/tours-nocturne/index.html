Why Wadi Rum

Your city is hiding 4,000 stars.

Street lights wash out all but the brightest stars in most cities. Our camp sits deep in the protected desert, 60 km from the nearest town, in air that is bone-dry most of the year. On a moonless night you see around 4,500 stars with the naked eye, and the Milky Way is bright enough to cast a shadow on the sand.
