    When to go

    Plan around the moon and the season.

    We run every night of the year. The sky is darkest in the few days around each new moon, so those dates fill first.

      What to bring

      • A warm layer, even in summer. The desert cools fast after sunset.
      • Closed shoes for walking on rock and sand.
      • A hat and jacket from November to March. We keep spare blankets at camp.
      • Your passport or ID for the park gate.
      • Cash for tips and the camp shop. Cards work for your booking.
