Our lab

Two Italians, one Portuguese grandmother and a very loud machine.

Giulia trained at the Carpigiani Gelato University in Bologna. Tiago grew up above his avó's pastelaria on Rua dos Remédios. In 2019 they opened Frolla in her old shop, kept her marble counter, and started churning.

Every batch is 6 litres. When a flavour runs out, it's gone until tomorrow — that's how you know it was made today.
