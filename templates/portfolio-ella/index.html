About Me

Hi, I'm Ella — I Photograph Real Moments, Not Stiff Poses

I picked up my dad's old film camera at fifteen and never put it down. Eleven years and more than three hundred weddings later, I still get butterflies at every first look.

My style is natural and documentary with a soft, bright finish that won't date. I'll gently guide you when it helps, then step back and let your day unfold — so you can enjoy it, and relive it for years.
