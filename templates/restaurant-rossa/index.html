How we make it

Slow dough. Fast fire.

  1. 48 h

    The dough rests

    Caputo flour, sea salt and a pinch of yeast, left to ferment for two days. It's why the crust is light, airy and easy on your stomach.

  2. 6 am

    Cheese is pulled

    Our fior di latte is stretched by hand every morning at a dairy in Red Hook, eight minutes from the shop.

  3. 90 s

    Into the fire

    Oak logs keep the oven floor at 485°C. Ninety seconds and one turn of the peel is all a pizza needs.
