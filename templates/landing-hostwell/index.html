FAQ
Hosting questions, answered
Can't find an answer? Our team is on live chat 24/7.

      

        
What is the renewal price?
The discounted price applies to your first term. After that, plans renew at the standard rate shown under each plan — for example, Premium renews at $8.99/mo. We email you 30 days before any renewal.

        
How does the 30-day money-back guarantee work?
If you cancel within 30 days, we refund the full hosting fee. Domain registrations are non-refundable, as they're paid to the registry.

        
Can I upgrade my plan later?
Yes, you can upgrade at any time from the control panel. You only pay the difference for the rest of your term, and there's no downtime.

        
Which payment methods do you accept?
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Google Pay and Apple Pay. Annual plans can also be paid by bank transfer.

        
Do you offer Windows hosting?
Our shared and cloud plans run on Linux. If you need Windows or .NET, choose a VPS plan with a Windows Server licence.
