The collection

Made by eleven hands.

Noor Haddad founded the house in 2016 in a former printworks in Gemmayzeh. Today eleven tailors and seamstresses cut, fit and finish every piece in the same room, with a second fitting studio on Rue de Grenelle in Paris.

A coat takes around forty hours. Buttonholes are sewn by hand. Hems are left generous so they can be let down years from now.
