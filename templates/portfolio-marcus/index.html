Common questions

Something else on your mind? Text me on (312) 555-0172.

I'm out of shape. Is that a problem?

Not at all — that's who I work with most. Your first sessions are about learning the movements and finding your starting point, not testing your limits.

How many sessions a week do I need?

Most clients do 2–3 sessions a week. With 2 coached sessions I'll give you a short plan for a third day on your own.

Do you give meal plans?

I give you calorie and protein targets and simple habits, and review your food log weekly. Strict meal plans rarely last, so we build on food you already like.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancel or move a 1:1 session up to 12 hours before at no cost. Class bookings can be cancelled up to 2 hours before.

Do session packs expire?

10-packs are valid for 4 months and 20-packs for 8 months, so there's room for holidays and busy weeks.
