Made in Porto

Two factories. Three generations. One tee.

We visit every month and pay 30% above the regional wage. Follow a Heavy Tee from yarn to your door.

  1. Day 1

    Knitted in Barcelos

    Organic yarn from a GOTS farm in Turkey is knitted into 240 gsm jersey at the Ferreira family mill.

  2. Day 4

    Cut and sewn in Porto

    Fourteen people at Costura Lima cut, sew and double-stitch every seam. Each one signs the tag.

  3. Day 6

    Washed 50 times

    A sample from every batch goes through 50 washes. If it shrinks more than 3%, the batch is rejected.

  4. Day 9

    At your door

    Packed in recycled paper and delivered carbon-neutral in two days across the EU, UK and US.
