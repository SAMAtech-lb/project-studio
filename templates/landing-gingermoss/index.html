Your kitten's first week

  1. Day 1 — Keep it quietStart with one room: bed, litter tray, food and water far apart.
  2. Days 2–3 — Same foodKeep the breeder's or shelter's food for a week, then switch slowly over 7 days.
  3. Days 3–5 — Play every dayTwo or three short wand-toy sessions help them settle and sleep at night.
  4. Day 7 — First vet visitBook vaccinations and a microchip check, or visit our Saturday clinic first.
