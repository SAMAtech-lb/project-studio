About

I work when everyone else goes home.

I'm Idris Qasem, a night-sky photographer from Amman, Jordan. I started by sleeping on my roof in Wadi Rum with a borrowed camera. Eleven years on, I shoot campaigns, film plates and magazine stories under the darkest skies I can reach.

Night work comes down to planning. I scout every location in daylight, plot the sky weeks ahead and build in spare nights for weather, so clients get the shot without paying for guesswork.
