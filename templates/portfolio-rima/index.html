From my brides

Good to know

How far ahead should I book?

Summer Saturdays book 9–12 months ahead. For weekdays and winter weddings, 3–4 months is usually fine. Check your date below to see if I'm free.

Do I need a trial?

Not required, but most brides love it. We try your look, take photos in daylight and adjust until it feels right. It's included in the Signature package or $150 on its own.

How long will the makeup last?

Bridal makeup is built to last 14+ hours with long-wear base and waterproof eye products. You'll get a lip and powder touch-up kit for the evening.

I have sensitive skin. Is that OK?

Yes. Tell me about allergies when you book and I'll use fragrance-free and non-comedogenic products. A trial is the best way to test.

What's your cancellation policy?

The 30% deposit is non-refundable, but it can be moved once to a new date within 12 months if I'm available.
