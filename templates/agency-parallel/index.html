    Questions we hear a lot.

    Something else? Ana answers every message herself within a day.

    How much does a typical project cost?

    Most online shops and booking platforms land between €60k and €180k, depending on integrations and how much is custom. The Discovery Sprint gives you a fixed number before you commit.

    Do we own the code and design files?

    Yes — everything lives in your GitHub and Figma from day one. No lock-in, no licence fees.

    Can you work with our in-house team?

    Often. We pair with in-house developers and designers, share rituals and hand over gradually so your team owns the product after launch.

    Which time zones do you cover?

    Lisbon and Berlin cover 7am–7pm CET. For US clients we run overlapping afternoons and a weekly call at a time that suits you.

    What happens after launch?

    30 days of aftercare are included. Most clients then move to a Growth Partner retainer so we keep shipping improvements every month.
