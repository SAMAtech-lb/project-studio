How it works

From first call to final inspection

  1. 01

    Free consultation

    We discuss the project, site and budget. I send a fixed-fee proposal within 2 working days.

  2. 02

    Site visit & survey

    I visit the site, review soils reports and existing drawings, and confirm the design loads.

  3. 03

    Design & drawings

    Calculations, structural models and a sealed drawing set, coordinated with your architect.

  4. 04

    Construction support

    Answers to contractor questions, site observations and the final engineer's letter.
