Your first appointment

You leave the first hour with a diagnosis and a plan.

Wear something you can move in. Bring any scan reports or surgery letters. If you're claiming on insurance, bring your authorisation number.

  1. 0–15 minYour story

    When it started, what makes it worse, what you need to get back to — work, sport, sleep.

  2. 15–35 minExamination

    Movement, strength and nerve tests to find the source, not just where it hurts.

  3. 35–53 minFirst treatment

    Hands-on treatment, acupuncture or exercise — so you feel a change before you leave.

  4. 53–60 minYour plan

    Exercises sent to your phone and how many sessions to expect.

    Questions

    Before you book

    Something else? Call us on 020 7123 4580 — a physio, not a call centre, will answer.

    Do I need a referral from my GP?

    No. You can book directly. Some insurers ask for a GP referral before they authorise treatment — check with them first if you're claiming.

    How many sessions will I need?

    It depends on the problem, but most people need between 3 and 6. We'll give you an estimate at the end of your first appointment and won't book more than you need.

    What should I wear?

    Loose, comfortable clothes. Shorts for knee, hip or ankle problems; a vest top for shoulder and neck problems. We have changing rooms.

    What's your cancellation policy?

    Cancel or move your appointment for free up to 24 hours before. Later cancellations are charged at 50%.

    Do you offer scans?

    Our sports medicine doctor can refer you for an MRI or ultrasound scan, usually within the same week, at a partner imaging centre in Clapham.
