Frequently asked questions

Is the Starter plan really free?

Yes. You can invoice up to 3 clients for free, forever. You only pay the standard card fee when a client pays by card.

How do card payments and fees work?

Payments are processed by Stripe. Fees start at 1.4% + 20¢ for European cards and 2.9% + 30¢ for other cards. Bank transfers are free. Money arrives in your account in 2 working days.

Can I move my clients from another tool?

Yes. Import clients and past invoices from a CSV file, or directly from FreshBooks, Wave and QuickBooks.

Does Tallybook work for my country's tax rules?

Tallybook supports VAT, GST and sales tax, multiple currencies and invoice numbering rules in 60 countries. Your accountant can get read-only access.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from your settings and you'll move to the free plan at the end of your billing period. Your invoices stay available to download.
