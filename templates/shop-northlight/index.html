Poured in Leith, by four people.

Isla started Northlight at her kitchen table in 2017 after one too many candles that smelled like nothing once lit. Today the four of us pour around 600 candles a week, in batches of 40, in a small studio off Constitution Street.

We test every scent for two weeks before it goes on sale, in real rooms, at real temperatures. If it doesn't fill a living room by the second hour, it doesn't make the cut.
