Our Story

A Café Built on Good Coffee & Kind Faces

Bean & Bloom started as a coffee cart outside Bath Abbey with one grinder, one kettle and a tin of homemade flapjacks. Ten years later we're a little café with a big oven, but the idea hasn't changed.

We serve coffee from small roasters in the South West, bake everything in-house with local flour and butter, and remember your order by your second visit.
