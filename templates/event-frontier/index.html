FAQ

Good to know

Can I get a refund?

Tickets are fully refundable until 12 October 2026, and transferable to a colleague at any time.

Will the talks be recorded?

Yes — every main-stage talk is recorded and available to all ticket holders within 48 hours.

Do you offer student or diversity tickets?

We reserve 200 free tickets for students and under-represented groups. Applications open in August.

Is the venue accessible?

Fully step-free, with live captioning on the main stage and a quiet room on every floor.
