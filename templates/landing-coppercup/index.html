Our story

From a tiny cart to three cafés in the city

Copper Cup started as a coffee cart outside the Ferry Building in 2014. Today we roast our own beans in the Mission, bake every pastry in-house and serve around 1,800 cups a day across three cafés.

We buy directly from six farms in Ethiopia, Colombia and Guatemala, pay well above Fair Trade prices and visit every harvest.
