The hotel

A family hotel since 1971, rebuilt around the view.

The Ferrer family opened Azure Bay with twelve rooms and a fishing boat. Today it has 48, but the idea hasn't changed: every room faces the sea, the fish comes from the harbour below, and you'll be greeted by name by the second morning.
