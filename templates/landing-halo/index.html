Switching to Halo

Switch in 10 minutes.

Can I keep my number?

Yes. Text "PAC" to 50123 from your current network, add the code to your order and we move your number within one working day.

Do I need to cancel my old contract?

No. When your number moves to Halo, your old network closes your account automatically. Check if you have early-exit fees left.

eSIM or physical SIM?

Both are free. eSIM arrives by email in minutes; a physical SIM arrives next working day by An Post.

Is there a credit check?

For 12-month SIM-only plans and phones, yes — a soft check that doesn't affect your credit score. 30-day plans need no credit check.
