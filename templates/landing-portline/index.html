    FAQ

    Before you book.

    What's the difference between FCL and LCL?

    FCL (full container load) means you rent a whole 20' or 40' container. LCL (less than container load) means your pallets share a container with other shippers — cheaper below about 13 m³, but 4–6 days slower because of consolidation.

    Do you handle customs clearance?

    Yes. We're a licensed customs broker in the Netherlands, Germany, Poland and the UAE, and work with vetted partners everywhere else. Add it to any quote for a fixed $185 per declaration.

    Can you ship dangerous goods or temperature-controlled cargo?

    We move IMO-classified goods by sea and road, and run reefer containers from −25 °C to +25 °C with live temperature logs on the tracking page.

    How accurate is the instant quote?

    For standard cargo, within ±5% of the final invoice. Oversized cargo, peak-season surcharges and remote pickups are confirmed by your account manager before booking.

    What happens if my shipment is delayed?

    You'll get an email and SMS the moment the ETA moves by more than 12 hours, with the reason and the new plan. Your account manager calls you for anything over 48 hours.
