# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Small-business owners and freelancers (restaurants, clinics, shops, designers, consultants) who buy a ready-made website from the studio and rebrand it with their own name, photos and logo. The studio owner uses the dashboard to show prospects the library and pick a starting template.

## Product Purpose

A library of complete, commercial-quality website templates (ThemeForest level) shown in the studio dashboard. Success: a prospect looks at a template and believes it is a real, finished site they would pay for.

## Positioning

Every template is a full, believable site with real-looking content and at least one genuinely working feature (filters, cart, booking, lightbox), not a demo shell.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Each template is plain `index.html` + `style.css` + one inline script. No frameworks, no build step.
- Allowed CDNs: Google Fonts, Remix Icon, Devicon; GSAP/Lenis only when the design needs it.
- Registered in `templates.js`; shown in the dashboard by category.
- Photos from Unsplash.

## Brand Commitments

- Fictional brands only; house fictional clients: Northwind, Lumen, Orbit, Fieldnote, Arcade, Voltra.
- E-commerce templates may show real branded products under a fictional store name.
- Owner's taste (confirmed feedback): bright, standard, commercial layouts; no dark-luxury black + gold serif; no gimmick widgets (price sliders, calculators, toggles); nothing that looks "vibe coded" (emoji icons, gradient text, glow blobs, bobbing cards).

## Evidence on Hand

No real client content. All copy, names and testimonials are authored demonstration content for a fictional brand.

## Product Principles

1. Looks like a real, finished site a client would buy.
2. Easy to rebrand: one accent token, swappable photos.
3. Each new template differs clearly from its siblings.
4. Working features over decorative ones.
